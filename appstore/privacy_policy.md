# Privacy Policy — Nodus

Last updated: 2026-05-07

---

## Overview

Nodus ("the app") is a note-taking app developed by Kyota Nakada ("I", "me").
This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

---

## Data Collection

**Nodus does not collect any personal data.**

Specifically:
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No account registration required
- No data is sent to any server

---

## Data Storage

All notes are stored as plain Markdown files (`.md`) in iCloud Drive, in a folder you select on first launch.

- **iCloud Drive**: Files are synced via Apple's iCloud service. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud storage and sync. See [apple.com/privacy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/).
- **Your device**: App preferences (default editor mode, selected folder bookmark) are stored locally using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults).

---

## Data Sharing

I do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party.

The only external service used at runtime is **cdnjs.cloudflare.com**, from which the app loads the marked.js library for Markdown rendering in preview mode. This request does not include any note content or personal information.

---

## Children's Privacy

Nodus does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not collect any personal data from any user.
